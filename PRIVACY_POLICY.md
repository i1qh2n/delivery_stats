# Privacy Policy for Delivery Stats

**Last Updated: August 9, 2026**

Route ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This
Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when
you use the Delivery Stats mobile application ("App").

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using the App to track
your delivery performance and logistics.

* **Performance Data**: Information about your delivery runs, including stop
  counts, miles driven, time spent, and efficiency metrics.
* **Logistics Data**: Information regarding your delivery routes and the trucks
  or vehicles used.
* **User Notes**: Any text-based notes or reminders you enter regarding routes,
  runs, or general activities.
* **Attachments**: Files, photos, or documents you choose to attach to your
  routes or delivery runs.

### 2. Permissions and Sensitive Information

To provide its core features, the App requires access to the following:

* **Location Services**: The App uses your device's GPS to geotag attachments
  (photos or files). This provides spatial context to your delivery data.
  Location data is processed locally on your device.
* **Storage Access**: The App requires permission to read from and write to your
  device's storage. This is used for:
    * Importing JSON backups to restore your database.
    * Exporting JSON backups, Excel reports, and PDF reports to your device.

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

* To provide and maintain the App's tracking and visualization features.
* To generate historical performance reports and efficiency metrics.
* To allow you to manage and organize your delivery routes and fleet.
* To enable data portability via import and export features.

### 4. Data Storage and Third Parties

**Local Storage**: All data, including performance metrics, notes, and
attachments, is stored locally on your device within an internal database and
file system.

**No Third-Party Sharing**: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your
information to third parties. Your data remains on your device unless you choose
to export it or share it using your device's native sharing capabilities.

**Third-Party Services**: The App may use standard system-level services (like
Google Play Services on Android) for basic functionality. These services have
their own privacy policies.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your data is stored locally, you have full control over it:

* You can delete specific entries (runs, notes, attachments) within the App.
* You can clear all App data via your device settings.
* Uninstalling the App will generally delete all local data associated with it,
  unless you have exported backups to external storage.

### 6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the security of your local data. However,
please be aware that the security of data stored on your device also depends on
your own device security settings (e.g., screen locks, encryption).

### 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any
changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last
Updated" date.
